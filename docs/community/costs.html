What GoodPush costs¶

GoodPush is free and will stay free: no ads, no tracking, no accounts, no paid tier. It runs on your computer with your own free sign-ins to Garmin, Strava or Google, so there are no servers to pay for.

A few things that would make GoodPush easier and safer to install do cost money. This page lists each one, what it costs and what it would change for you. None of them are in place yet.

What would cost money¶

Prices as published in November 2026, in US dollars; they change, and this table is updated when they do.

What

What it changes for you

Cost

Mac: Apple Developer Program

The Mac app is notarised: no “can’t be verified” warning, no trip to Privacy & Security. Also needed for any iPhone app.

$99 a year

Windows: code signing

The installer carries a verified publisher instead of “Unknown publisher”, and the “Windows protected your PC” warning fades as the signature builds a reputation.

free to $300 a year (below)

Android: Google Play account

Needed to publish an Android app on Google Play.

$25 once

A web address (e.g. goodpush.app)

A short, permanent address for the guide and downloads. Nice to have.

about $15 a year

Windows signing, cheapest first. SignPath Foundation signs open-source projects for free. Otherwise: Microsoft’s Artifact Signing (about $10 a month, only for individuals in the USA and Canada) or a certificate from a certificate authority ($150-300 a year).

Phone apps don’t exist yet. GoodPush already works in your phone’s browser (goodpush app --lan). A real Android or iPhone app is a lot of work beyond the store fees, so those costs only matter once someone builds one. They’re listed so that you can see the whole picture.

First year, everything: $139 with free Windows signing, up to $439 with a paid certificate. After that: $114-414 a year.

Questions about any of this: open an issue.
