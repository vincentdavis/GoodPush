GoodPush¶

Free cycling-training analysis and an adaptive training plan that run on your own computer. Connect your Garmin (or drop in a Strava or Garmin export) and GoodPush shows what you can do now, how your training has gone, how your body is doing, and what to ride next. Everything runs locally: your data never leaves your computer.

Beta: expect bugs

GoodPush is in beta testing. Things will break, numbers can be off, and settings may change between versions. Please tell us what goes wrong, and whether the numbers make sense for you. GoodPush is not medical advice: see the disclaimer.

New here? Install it, then get your data in. The rest of the guide explains each page of the app; you don’t need to read it all.

Want to help? Most of the help GoodPush needs is no code at all: using it and saying what goes wrong, and saying whether its numbers match what you know about your body. See Contributing. GoodPush is free; What it costs lists what would cost money, such as code signing and app stores.
